    Questions

    Before you ask.

    Is a status a buy or sell signal?

    No. A status describes the last hour of trading, nothing more. Coins go from Flatline to Elevated and back. VITALS never says buy or sell.

    Where does the data come from?

    Checkups and the Ward read DexScreener’s public API. New launches come from pump.fun’s public launch stream through PumpPortal. Your browser fetches both directly. No account, no keys, no tracking.

    Why does a brand-new coin say “No chart”?

    DexScreener needs a moment to pick up a new pair. Give it a minute and take its vitals again.

    How exact is the time of death?

    It is the moment this page first saw no trades for 5 minutes, checking each bed about every 20 seconds. Close, not forensic.

    Can a coin come back from a flatline?

    Sometimes. If it trades again, the Ward clears the time of death and gives it its tag back.

    Where do the creator fees go?

    Into the night shift first: the Code Blue bot and alerts, which have to run around the clock. Then buybacks and development, in the split shown above.

    Will VITALS DM me?

    Never. There is no presale, no airdrop and no support account. Anyone messaging you as VITALS is not us.
